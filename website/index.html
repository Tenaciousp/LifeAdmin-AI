Before you begin

A few sensible questions.

Is the app available to buy now?

LifeAdmin AI is in final testing. This website introduces the product and its planned UK launch price. Purchase access will follow completed testing; no payment is collected here.

Does LifeAdmin contact providers for me?

No. The app prepares a plan, a message draft and a checklist. You review, edit and send messages yourself. You also approve any cancellation, switch or purchase.

Does the app connect to my bank or email?

No bank or mailbox access is required for the core workflow. Add only the details relevant to your household task.

What if some bill details are missing?

The guided flow works with the information you provide and highlights useful details to check. Unknown prices, dates or contract terms should be verified with your provider.

Is there a subscription or an upgrade fee?

The planned offer is one complete package for a one-time payment. There is no planned monthly subscription or separate feature upgrade.

Is this professional advice?

LifeAdmin provides general information, plans and drafts. For legal, financial, medical, tax or insurance decisions, use official information and qualified support where appropriate.
