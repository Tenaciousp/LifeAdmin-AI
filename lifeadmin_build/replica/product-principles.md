# HomeAdmin distinctiveness and final-draft constraints
Baseline: merged PR #15 plus the separate PR #16 improvements. Existing code, bill records, reviewed results and work in progress are preserved.
## Product promise
Manage household bills and subscriptions with practical bill-specific plans, letters and replies, without requiring a HomeAdmin account or bank connection.
## Preserve through redesign
- Household-wide individual bill categories, including separate Energy and Water with relevant fields and guidance.
- Local bill/result storage and existing backup compatibility; clearly explain optional calendar/provider sharing.
- Preferred external AI choice, default Copilot. Integrated AI is optional and needs verified service availability plus explicit consent before selected facts are sent.
- Per-bill plan, letter and provider-reply results, source links, uncertainty checks and user review before saving/action.
- Follow-up and contract dates linked to each bill. Admin completion is distinct from payment confirmation.
- Currency-separated totals and country-aware facts. Do not imply full UI localisation or live offer verification from AI language options.
- Existing HomeAdmin/TenaciousPL branding and approved commercial model. Do not introduce subscription pricing, account requirements or banking access through a design change.
## Adopt selectively from competitors
Learn concise overviews, quick entry, clear date priority, readable costs and fewer steps. Implement original presentation and copy. Do not import logos, proprietary code, partner inventories or unverified savings claims.
## Final-draft direction
Home for summaries and next actions; Bills for search/list/detail; Plans & messages for reviewed outputs; Diary for dates; Settings for AI preferences/calendar setup/backups/support. Refactor progressively and preserve unsaved work and keyboard focus.
## Quality gate
Overall 9/10 remains an evidence-based target across presentation, ease, functionality, AI usefulness, reliability/accessibility and value. Automated scans, store ratings and feature parity do not establish that score. Keep live AI and installed-device evaluation open until completed.
## Tool use
Replica Review for research and preservation; Playwright/axe for regression evidence; Context7 for current technical documentation. Firecrawl is pending user connection and is restricted to authorised public-source research. Further skills require the user's agreement before installation.
